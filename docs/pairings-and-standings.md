# Pairings and Standings

## Pairing

Round 1 pairings are randomized — or a custom seating/playing order, when a draft's physical seating matters (see the seating chart on the Pairings page). From round 2 onward, pairing is Swiss-standard within the pod: entrants are grouped by their current record and paired within those groups, and two entrants who have already played each other in that pod are never paired together again.

On top of that pod-local hard-avoid, there's a soft avoidance across the whole tournament: if the same weekend has multiple pods (e.g. a Friday draft, a Saturday draft, a chaos draft), pairing tries to avoid rematching two entrants who already played each other in an *earlier* pod that weekend too. It's a preference, not a hard rule — with enough pods and a small enough player pool it can't always be honored — but it's what makes "everyone plays everyone" actually happen across a whole weekend, instead of the same two people drawing each other every event.

Before a round is started, an organizer can hand-pair it from scratch, or fix up the automatic pairing by clicking two entrants to swap their seats. Once a round is started, its pairings are locked.

## Standings

Per-pod standings use the standard Swiss tiebreakers, checked in order: match points, then Opponents' Match Win % (OMW%), then Game Win % (GW%), then Opponents' Game Win % (OGW%).

Intentional draws in a final game — agreeing to split rather than play out a meaningless match — are supported as a manual override on a match's result, so the standings math accounts for them rather than treating them as an anomaly.

## Tournament-wide standings (Gesamtwertung)

Across an entire tournament — which can span many pods over a weekend — the overall ranking is by **average match points per pod played**, not raw total points. Someone who only made it to 2 of the weekend's 4 pods is ranked fairly against someone who played all 4, rather than being penalized just for missing events.

Team pods (e.g. 2HG) credit each member the team's full match points — points aren't divided between teammates.
